VISIONGUARD EDGE

Affordable Offline AI Quality Inspection for Indian MSMEs

Turn any off-the-shelf camera or webcam into an offline AI visual inspection system. Designed specifically for retrofitting into Indian workshop floors for under ₹5,000.

Offline Edge Inference

Runs fully locally on existing laptop CPUs without cloud dependence, protecting sensitive factory production data and ensuring zero internet latency.

Deterministic Hybrid AI

Combines standard YOLOv8 object detection with sub-pixel contour checking to detect missing, rotated, or damaged board components in real-time.

Quality Records & PDFs

Automatically logs inspections to SQLite and generates professional PDF audit reports complete with annotations and action items.
